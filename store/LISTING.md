## Description (detailed)
```
Your credit card reports whatever balance it shows on the statement CLOSING date — not
your due date. Pay by the due date and you avoid interest, but the high balance was
already reported to the credit bureaus weeks earlier.

Statement Reminder makes the closing date impossible to miss:

• Add each card's closing day (just a nickname and a day of the month — never an
  account number or login)
• The toolbar badge counts down the days to your next closing date
• Get a notification 3 days before each card closes — enough time for a payment
  to post

Pay your balance down BEFORE the snapshot and your card reports low utilization,
which is one of the biggest factors in your credit score.

Private by design: no account, no server, no tracking. Everything you enter stays in
your browser (synced between your own devices by Chrome itself).

Want the full math? The free calculator at https://ababa1326.github.io/reportslow/
tells you exactly how much to pay down to hit your target utilization.
```

## Privacy tab
- **Single purpose:** Tracks credit-card statement closing days and reminds the user
  before each card's balance is reported to the credit bureaus.
- **storage justification:** Saves the card nicknames and closing days the user enters.
- **alarms justification:** Runs a once-daily check to refresh the badge countdown and
  decide whether a reminder is due.
- **notifications justification:** Shows the heads-up notification 3 days before a
  card's statement closes.
- **Data usage:** Does not collect or transmit any user data. All data stays in
  chrome.storage.sync. (Check "none of the above" / no data collected.)
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/ababa1326/statement-reminder/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
