# Privacy Policy — Statement Reminder

**Effective: June 12, 2026**

Statement Reminder is a Chrome extension that tracks credit-card statement closing days
and reminds you before each card's balance is reported to the credit bureaus.

## What the extension stores
The card nicknames and closing days you enter. That's all. This data is saved with
Chrome's built-in `storage.sync`, which keeps it in your browser and, if you're signed
into Chrome, syncs it between your own devices via your Google account.

## What the extension collects or transmits
**Nothing.** The extension has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and makes no
network requests. Your data never leaves your browser, and the developer never sees it.
No account numbers, balances, or credentials are ever requested — only a nickname and a
day of the month.

## Permissions
- **storage** — save the cards you add
- **alarms** — run a once-a-day check to update the badge countdown
- **notifications** — show a heads-up 3 days before a card closes

## Removing your data
Remove cards in the popup, or uninstall the extension — Chrome deletes its storage.
